Our Faculties

Busitema Campus, Busia District

Faculty of Engineering and Technology

Our original engineering faculty, based at the former National College of Agricultural Mechanization. It trains engineers and technologists, including in mining, electrical and computer, industrial and agro-processing engineering.

Nagongera Campus, Tororo District

Faculty of Science and Education

Focuses on science education and ICT in education, preparing teachers and scientists for schools and communities.

Research: education for sustainable development, astronomy, material science and ICT for education.

Arapai Campus, Soroti

Faculty of Agriculture and Animal Sciences

Covers crop production, animal production and agribusiness, with a strong practical and farm-based approach.

Research: livestock and poultry improvement and mushroom production.

Namasagali Campus, Kamuli District

Faculty of Natural Resources and Environmental Sciences

Studies how to manage natural resources and protect the environment, supporting sustainable livelihoods across the region.

Mbale Campus (Mbale City) and Kabwangasi Campus

Faculty of Health Sciences

A key centre for medical education and health research, training health professionals to serve communities across Eastern Uganda.

Research: malaria, ICT in health, and maternal and child health.

Pallisa Campus, Pallisa District

Faculty of Management Sciences

Focuses on management science, tourism and hospitality, preparing graduates for business, public service and enterprise.

Research: business survival, decentralisation, and organisational behaviour.

Teaching

Our programmes combine classroom learning with workshops, laboratories, field visits and community projects, so graduates leave with skills employers and communities can use.

Research and Outreach

Staff and students carry out research and outreach that respond to local needs, from smart agriculture and renewable energy to health and environmental sustainability.
